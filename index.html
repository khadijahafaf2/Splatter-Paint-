Recipe #1

  • Time:8-10 Miniutes
  • Serves:24
Ingredients

1.1 cup of salted butter

2.1 cup of granulated sugar

3.1 cup of light brown sugar packed

4.2 Teaspoons of vanilla extract

5.2 large eggs

6.3 cups of all-purpose flour

7.1 teaspoon of baking soda

8.1/2 teaspoon of baking powder

9.1 teaspoon of salt

10.2 cups of semisweet chocolate chips (12oz)

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line 3 baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. In a medium bowl,mix flour,baking soda,baking powder and salt. Set aside.
  3. Cream together butter and suger until combined.
  4. Beat in eggs and vanilla until light (about 1 minute).
  5. Mix in the dry ingredients.
  6. Add chocolate chips and mix well.
  7. Roll 2-3 Tablespoons(depending on how large you likeyour cookies) of dough at a time into balls and place then evenly spaced on your prepared cookies sheets.
  8. Bake in Preheated oven for approximately 8-10 minutes. Take them out when they are just barely starting to turn brown.
  9. Let them sit on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before removing to cool rack

Now you have your delicious chocolate chip cookies! 🍪
